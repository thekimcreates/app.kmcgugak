Privacy and account removal

Deployment starter: the organization must review and approve this notice before launching the web app.

This member app uses Firebase Authentication for member sign-in, Firestore for member profiles and conversations, and Firebase Storage for chat attachments. GitHub Pages hosts the app’s public interface.

Member information

The app uses your assigned member ID, display name, profile photo, and performance assignments. Password changes are handled by Firebase Authentication. Your password is not stored in chat messages or app source files.

Conversations

Team Chat is available to signed-in members. Direct and group conversations are limited by Firebase security rules to their participants. Chat moderators can remove messages where their permissions allow and review submitted reports. This app does not implement end-to-end encryption.

On this device

Sign-in persistence, preferences, and text drafts may be stored in your browser. Signing out clears the app’s visible private conversations and this account’s unsent text drafts. The service worker caches the public app interface, not private chat messages or attachments.

Removal and contact

To request account or content removal, contact kmcgugak.org@gmail.com. The organization must define and publish its retention and removal policy before wider deployment. Removing a chat message hides its content; uploaded objects may remain in storage until an administrator removes them.

Third-party services

Firebase, GitHub Pages, map providers, and linked media services may process information under their own policies. The organization is responsible for configuring access rules and explaining its practices to members and parents.
